Why we started it

The short version

Every November the same thing happens in Christchurch. A few hundred flats all need a cleaner, a trailer and a storage unit inside the same fortnight. Every contractor in town is booked solid. Students end up ringing round in the middle of exams, and the work gets done late, badly, or at eleven at night by the flat themselves.

Daniel had watched it happen enough times to think it could be done better — line the contractors up properly, deal with the scheduling, and let people get on with their study. He started FlatOut in September 2026. Rocky, a mate of his, came on board a few weeks later to take over the accounts, and that's where we are now.

We'll be straight with you: we're new. Starting in September meant we were late for most of this year's move-out season, so we've done a handful of flats rather than a hundred. This summer is our first proper run at it. What that means for you is that you're not a job number, and we can't afford a bad one.

Who turns up

The people doing the work

Every contractor we use is a local Christchurch business on a signed agreement with us. We require them to carry their own insurance and show us the certificate before they take a job.

Your agreement is with FlatOut, not with them. So if something turns up broken or a job isn't up to scratch, you call us and we deal with it. You're not stuck chasing a contractor you've never met.

What that means for you

  • Local businesses, not a labour-hire pool
  • Insurance certificate sighted before they take a job
  • One agreement, with us
  • Something goes wrong, you call one number
