Leaving a hall

Moving out of a hall of residence

Halls are a different job to flats. There's no landlord inspection and usually no clean to organise — but there is a hard closing date, a room full of things that won't fit in a car, and a few hundred other people trying to do the same thing in the same week.

What most people in halls actually need is somewhere to put their gear over summer, a run to the tip for what isn't worth keeping, and a lift for the rest. We'll do the lot in one visit, and if a few of you on the same floor go in together it works out cheaper for everyone.
