What happens next

  1. 1

    We call you

    Usually the same day. We'll sort a time to come and walk through the job with you — it takes about fifteen minutes.

  2. 2

    We look at the job

    One of us comes to the flat and sees what's actually there. You'll get an estimate on the spot so you know roughly where you stand.

  3. 3

    You get a written quote

    Once we've locked in the people doing the work, we send you one price covering everything. Nothing is charged until you accept it.

  4. 4

    We run the job

    We book the contractors, arrange access and keep it on track. You don't have to chase anyone.

What it costs, and when

The visit, the estimate and the quote are free. You're charged nothing until you've accepted a written quote from us.

Jobs up to $500

Paid in full two days before the job starts.

Jobs over $500

Half up front, three business days before the job starts — or at booking if you're booking inside that window. The rest is due fourteen days later.

One price, one invoice

Whether it's a clean, or a clean plus a truck plus a skip, you get a single price from us. You don't pay the contractors separately.

Tracking it all

Once you've accepted a quote we'll send you an invite to our customer portal, where your quote, invoice and payment history live in one place.

If it's not paid on time

The job doesn't go ahead. Overdue balances pick up a $30 admin fee and 2% a month.

These are the terms you'll be agreeing to when you accept the quote. We'd rather you saw them now than at the bottom of an invoice.

While you wait

Do I need to be there for the visit?

Yes, or someone from the flat does. We need to see the rooms, the access and what's going. Fifteen minutes is usually plenty.

What if the job's bigger than what I put in the form?

That's exactly what the visit is for, and it's why we don't quote off a form. The price reflects what we actually see. If it's more than you expected you can walk away — you haven't committed to anything.

Can I change what I need?

Yep, right up until you accept the quote. Tell us at the visit, or call us. Adding a skip or dropping the storage is no problem.

Do I have to be there on the day?

Usually not. Most students have already left. We arrange access and whoever needs to be on site, and we'll sort the details with you before the day.

Who actually turns up?

Local Christchurch contractors we've signed and checked. Your agreement is with us though — if anything goes wrong, you call us, not them.

Can my flatmates be on it too?

That's the normal way it works. One person organises, we quote the whole flat, and how you split it between you is up to you.

I need to change or cancel my request.

Call or text 027 408 6895 with your reference number, or email admin@flatoutnz.co.nz. No cost to cancel before you've accepted a quote.

Need us sooner?

November gets busy. If your handover date is close, say so — call or text and we'll move you up.
