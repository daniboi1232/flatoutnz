Questions

My flat wants to split the cost. Am I on the hook for all of it?

The quote and the invoice go to one person, because we need one name to send things to. How the flat divides it up is entirely yours to sort — most just transfer their share to whoever's name is on it. The person on the invoice is the one we'd come back to if it went unpaid, so pick someone who's happy with that.

Does it cost me anything?

The visit and the quote are free. The work itself you pay for — one price covering everything, paid to us rather than to four separate businesses. Nothing is charged until you've accepted a quote.

Who actually does the work?

Local Christchurch businesses we've contracted and checked. Your agreement is with us — we scope the job, quote it and manage the people doing it, so if something goes wrong you come to us.

Can I organise it for my whole flat?

Yes, and most people do. One person fills in the form for everyone. You can also use it just for your own stuff.

What areas do you cover?

Christchurch, with most of our work around Ilam, Riccarton, Upper Riccarton, Addington and the surrounding student suburbs.

How soon will I hear back?

We typically respond within the same day, and no later than the end of the following day.

What if I only need one thing?

That's fine. Plenty of people just need a cleaner, or just need a couch gone. Tick one box and that's all you'll hear about.
