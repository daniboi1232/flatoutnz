Privacy

How FlatOut handles your information

This website (flatoutnz.netlify.app) is a static site. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't run any tracking scripts, and doesn't store anything you do here.

When you tap "Get a Quote" or "Landlord Enquiries," you're taken to a form hosted by Google Forms — FlatOut doesn't build or host that form itself. Whatever you submit there is governed by Google's privacy policy, and FlatOut receives your responses in order to connect you with suitable local moving, cleaning, rubbish and storage providers in Christchurch.

FlatOut doesn't sell your information or share it beyond what's needed to arrange the services you've asked about.
